InnerPause

The Problem

Calming down is especially hard with ADHD, when thoughts keep racing. Wellness experiences that ask for too much effort or attention can feel out of reach in exactly those moments.

Key Features

01

Emotion Check-In

Users choose what they are carrying right now: Anxious, Overwhelmed, Lonely, or Angry.

02

Gentle Grounding

An interactive 3-3-3 grounding exercise and a gentle reminder, with options for Suggestions, a Short break, or a video.

03

Mindful Breaks

A short meditation timer with 15, 30, 60, or 180 second options keeps breaks manageable.

04

Emotional Reflection

After an activity, a simple happy or sad choice asks, “Feel a little lighter now?”
